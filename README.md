Fleet Fuel & Expense Manager is a tool for tracking fuel use and running costs across a fleet of vehicles. It runs entirely in your browser—no installation, no server, no account needed. Just open the page. Start using it. Your data is saved automatically in your browser’s storage so it stays private and accessible whenever you open the app.

The app has four tabs. The Dashboard gives a look at key figures. It shows summary cards a chart of fuel economy by vehicle (in km/L) a chart of monthly fuel cost trends in rupees and a table that lists all vehicles with their latest details. The Vehicles tab lets you add, edit or deactivate vehicles. You can record the registration number, make and model vehicle type, fuel type and add notes. Deactivating a vehicle keeps all its history intact so you can still view past data.

The Refuelling tab is where you log each fill-up. You enter the date and time odometer reading, litres filled price per litre and the total cost. The app calculates fuel economy using the tank-to-full-tank method. It measures distance driven since the full tank and divides it by the litres bought since then. This method gives km/L and cost-per-km values even if some fill-ups are partial.

The Expenses tab lets you record all costs like repairs, maintenance, tyres, insurance, tolls, parking and other expenses. All data is protected by checks. The app rejects zero entries blocks future dates and warns you if an odometer reading doesn’t fit between previous and next records. It also flags any receipt total that differs from litres × price by than 1%. The receipt total is filled in automatically until you type your own.

You can filter data by vehicle and date range. Reports can be exported to CSV files that open in Excel or printed directly from your browser.

The app includes demo data for five vehicles, each with twelve refuelling records and two expenses so you can try it away. A "Reset demo data" button brings the sample data back at any time.

It works well for businesses, delivery teams, families, with multiple cars or anyone who wants to see exactly where their fuel money goes.
